Covariation and correlation are closely related ideas in statistics, but they are not the same thing. Correlation is actually derived from covariation.
1️⃣ Covariation (Covariance)
Covariation (covariance) measures how two variables change together.
Key ideas
If X increases and Y increases together → positive covariance
If X increases and Y decreases → negative covariance
If changes are unrelated → covariance near 0
Example
Study Hours	Marks
2	50
4	60
6	70
As study hours increase, marks increase → positive covariance.
Limitations
Value depends on units of measurement
Hard to interpret directly (could be 200, 5000, etc.)
2️⃣ Correlation
Correlation is the standardized version of covariance.
It tells how strong the relationship is between variables.
Range
Correlation always lies between:
-1  ≤  r  ≤  1
Interpretation
Correlation	Meaning
+1	Perfect positive relationship
0	No relationship
-1	Perfect negative relationship
Example:
Height vs weight → positive correlation
Speed vs travel time → negative correlation
🔑 Main Differences
Feature	Covariation (Covariance)	Correlation
Meaning	Measures how variables vary together	Measures strength of relationship
Range	−∞ to +∞	−1 to +1
Units	Depends on units	Unitless
Interpretation	Harder	Easy
Formula	Mean product of deviations	Covariance normalized
✅ Simple intuition
Covariance → Direction of relationship
Correlation → Direction + Strength
✔️ Quick memory trick:
Covariance = raw relationship
Correlation = normalized relationship
